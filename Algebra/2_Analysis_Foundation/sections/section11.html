11. Reading Derivative Graphs Graphically

The Complete Graphical Synthesis Protocol

When handed a raw derivative graph f'(x), use this two-tier visual execution layout to systematically discover and plot every key landmark feature of the hidden parent function f(x).

STEP 1: Locate the Axis Intersections (Roots) f'(x) = 0
↳ Scan the horizontal axis to find where the derivative graph cuts through y = 0. These coordinates are the **Stationary Points** of your parent function. Crossing from below to above marks a local minimum; crossing from above to below marks a local maximum.
STEP 2: Extract Extrema from the Slopes (Peaks & Valleys) f''(x) = 0
↳ Locate every local peak (maximum) and valley (minimum) directly on the derivative curve. Because these positions represent where the slope of the derivative is flat, they map directly to the **Inflection Points** of the parent function, signaling an exact shift in concavity geometry.
